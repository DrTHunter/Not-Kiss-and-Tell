# Not Kiss and Tell

## The Story

Fardo was set to drop "Kiss and Tell" — a white girl anthem that had TikTok completely lit waiting for release. 

The clips that leaked were killer, and the hype was real. Fans were counting down the days.

But behind the scenes, they needed clearance to use portions of a Katy Perry song. 

## Katy Perry's punk ass label declined the request, and without that clearance, the song couldn't move forward as originally intended.

It's a shame the world won't get to hear it
